Microsoft: 50 years of computing.

Assembling the particle field...

  1. In 1975, Altair BASIC turned a few pages of code into Microsoft's first product.

  2. In the 1980s, the personal computer moved into everyday life.

  3. In the 1990s, Windows and the Internet connected people beyond the desktop.

  4. In the 2000s, enterprise work moved online, connecting teams, information, and decisions across the web.

  5. In the 2010s, enterprise systems moved into cloud datacenters built for global scale.

  6. In the 2020s, Microsoft powered intelligence across people, software, and enterprise systems.

  7. By 2025, Microsoft had spent fifty years turning ambitious ideas into everyday tools.

  8. 2026 and Beyond: AI can power an age of abundance, expanding agency, discovery, and possibility.

A history of computing is a history of becoming.
